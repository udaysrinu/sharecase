Someone shares a basket. You get your own buy list.

A basket is a list of stocks and target percentages, not share counts. Type what you want to invest and sharecase turns those percentages into exact whole-share orders at your capital, not theirs.

Open the workbench New to this? Read the 2-minute guide

Runs in your browser. No account. Nothing uploaded: only stock symbols go to this app's own /api/price. Not investment advice.

How it works

1 Load the sheet

Drag the .json a friend sent you onto the page, or paste the text. You immediately see how many stocks it holds and the minimum it needs.

2 Type your amount

Get share counts, total cost, and how far each holding lands from its target weight once rounded to whole shares.

3 Place the orders

Copy them into your broker by hand, or hand the whole basket to Zerodha Kite pre-filled. You review and confirm every order there.

The idea

You invest
₹4,00,000
StockSharesWeight
TCS3124.7%
RELIANCE9230.2%
HDFCBANK10345.1%
Your friend invests
₹80,000
StockSharesWeight
TCS624.1%
RELIANCE1829.7%
HDFCBANK2146.2%

Same file, same targets of 25 / 30 / 45%, and completely different share counts. That is the whole trick: percentages travel between people, share counts don't.

Notice the weights aren't identical either. Whole shares don't divide evenly, so the smaller amount drifts further from target: 1.2 points at ₹80,000 versus 0.3 at ₹4,00,000. The page shows you that drift rather than hiding it, and below this basket's minimum of ₹9,290 it stops you instead of quietly dropping TCS. (Illustrative prices.)

What it will not do

Send your holdings anywhere

Live prices come from this app's own /api/price, and only stock symbols go to it, never quantities, amounts or files. Or skip it and use the sheet's prices, or type them from your broker app.

Shrink a basket silently

Below the minimum, the priciest stock can't get even one share. Rather than drop it and hand you a different portfolio, the page blocks and suggests amounts that work.

Give you advice

It does arithmetic. It doesn't know whether a basket is any good, and nothing is placed until you confirm it yourself in your own broker.
